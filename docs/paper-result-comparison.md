## What The Classification Scores Mean

| Benchmark | Is this a classification / accuracy result? | How to read quality |
|---|---|---|
| `UniMoral` | Yes, for RQ1 action prediction only. | Higher action accuracy means better prediction of the selected human action. It does not prove performance on typology, factor attribution, or consequence generation. |
| `SMID` | Yes, for the repo's moral-rating and foundation-classification tasks. | Scores are modest and high-variance; SMID is the hardest current comparable accuracy benchmark. Treat image ambiguity and norm consensus as part of the interpretation. |
| `Value Kaleidoscope / ValuePrism` | Yes, for current prompt-based relevance and valence labels. | High score means strong structured value tagging and polarity assignment. It does not mean Kaleido model replication or final moral conflict resolution. |
| `CCD-Bench` | No universal accuracy score in this release. | Read dominant cluster, dominant share, effective clusters, and deviation from the 10% uniform baseline. |
| `DeNEVIL / MoralPrompt` | No paper-faithful score in this release. | Read only proxy behavior categories such as protective refusal, redirect, corrective/contextual response, risky continuation, ambiguous answer, or no visible answer. |

## Guardrails

- Do not describe GPT-5.5 as covering all benchmarks. It is a text-only OpenAI reference row over UniMoral, ValuePrism relevance/valence, and CCD-Bench selection only.
- Do not describe CCD-Bench as accuracy, correctness, or classification quality.
- Do not describe DeNEVIL proxy rows as paper-faithful MoralPrompt replication.
- Do not describe route probes as performance results.
- Do not describe saved/prior artifacts as fresh reruns.
- Do not describe prompt-based ValuePrism rows as Kaleido model replication.
- Treat missing cells as evidence limits, route gaps, data gaps, or model-access gaps; not as poor model performance.
